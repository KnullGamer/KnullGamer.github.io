Garlic Butter Rice

Servings: 6

Estimated Cost for 6 People: $4.50-$5.50 total

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups long-grain white rice (or jasmine rice), rinsed
  • 2.5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1.5-2 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1/4 cup yellow onion, finely chopped
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a large saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes until tender.
  3. Stir in the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the chicken broth, salt, and pepper and bring to a boil.
  5. Add the rinsed rice and stir once.
  6. Cover with a tight-fitting lid and reduce heat to low.
  7. Simmer for 15-18 minutes.
  8. Remove from heat and let rest for 5-10 minutes.
  9. Fluff the rice and stir in the remaining 3 tbsp butter.
  10. Serve warm.

Cilantro Lime Rice

Servings: 6

Estimated Cost for 6 People: $3.50-$4.50 total

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp lime zest
  • 3 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add the rice and stir to coat.
  3. Add water and salt and bring to a boil.
  4. Cover and simmer on low for 15-18 minutes.
  5. Remove from heat and let stand covered for 5 minutes.
  6. Fold in lime juice, lime zest, and cilantro.
  7. Fluff with a fork and serve.

Buttery Seasoned Rice

Servings: 6

Estimated Cost for 6 People: $4.00-$5.00 total

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 3.25 cups water
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tsp chicken bouillon powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 0.5 tsp dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp parsley

Directions

  1. Combine rice, water, bouillon powder, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, thyme, black pepper, and parsley in a saucepan.
  2. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
  3. Cover with a lid and reduce heat to medium-low.
  4. Cook for 13 minutes without lifting the lid.
  5. Remove from heat and let rest covered for 10 minutes.
  6. Stir in the butter.
  7. Fluff with a fork and serve.
