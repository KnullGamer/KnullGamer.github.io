Crispy Herb-Crusted Salmon (Pan-to-Oven)

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 6 people

Estimated Cost: $36.00 total (about $6.00 per serving)

Ingredients

  • 6 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
  • 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 1 tablespoon dried thyme
  • 1 tablespoon dried rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Preparation Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Combine panko breadcrumbs, oregano, thyme, rosemary, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and olive oil in a bowl.
  3. Brush the top of each salmon fillet with a thin layer of Dijon mustard.
  4. Press the breadcrumb mixture firmly onto the mustard-coated side of each fillet.
  5. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Place salmon skin-side down and sear for 2 minutes.
  7. Transfer the skillet to the preheated oven.
  8. Bake for 8–10 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the salmon is cooked through.
  9. Serve immediately and enjoy.
