Mashed Potatoes in the Body

The Story

This section will include how the mashed potatoes travel through the body and are digested.

  • The Mouth
    • Mastication (chewing) using the teeth
    • Salivary amylase (enzyme) from saliva breaks down the starch in the mashed potatoes (carbs) into disaccharides
    • Creates bolus

    • The Two Types of Digestion

    There are two types of digestion, mechanical digestion and chemical digestion. Mechanical Digestion happens in the Mouth, the Esophagus, the Pharynx, the Stomach, and the Small Intestine. Chemical Digestion happens in the Mouth with Saliva, the Stomach, and Small Intestine.

    Enzymes +Other

    • Amylase
    • Pepsin
    • Bile

    Amylase is produced in the salivary glands and is inside of your saliva. It is used to break down starch into more simple carbohydrates. Pepsin is an enzyme that is activated by low ph inside of the stomach. To activate the Pepsin, the Stomach uses Hydrochloric Acid to lower the ph inside of the Stomach. Bile is used to break down fat in the Small Intestine and it is released by the Gall Bladder.
