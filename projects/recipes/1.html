The Recipe

The Ingredients

A recipe by my great, great, grandmother

Ingredient Amount
Ground Chuck 1 pound
Chopped Celery 1 cup
Chopped Onion 1 cup
Stuffing or dried French Bread pieces 10 cups
Sage, Black Pepper, and Salt Season to taste
Chicken or Turkey Broth Up to four cups

Steps

  1. Cook Celery and onions in broth
  2. Add uncooked ground chuck
  3. Add stuffing or dried bread to desired consistency; do not make it to moist
  4. Simmer on stove, bake in oven or stuffed turkey
  5. Add seasonings to taste
