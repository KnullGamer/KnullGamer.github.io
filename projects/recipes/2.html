The Recipe

The Ingredient

A recipe by my grandmother.

Ingredient Amount
Choclate Cake Mix 1 package
Cherry Pie Filling 21 ounces
Vanilla Extract 1 teaspoon
Eggs 2
Sugar 1 cup
Butter 5 Tablespoons
Milk 1/3 cup
Choclate Chips 1 cup

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees farhenheit
  2. Mix the Cake Mix, Vannila Extract, Eggs, and Cherry Pie Filling by hand and bake in a 9x13 inch
  3. Bake for 25-30 minutes until cooked thorugh
  4. For the frosting, combine the Sugar, Butter, and Milk and boil and stir constanly for 1 minute
  5. Remove for heat and stir in choclate chips till chips are melted
  6. Pour frosting over cake while hot
