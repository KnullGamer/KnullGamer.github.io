Garlic & Herb Green Veggie Roast

Recipe Information

Recipe Details
Prep Time Cook Time Servings Estimated Cost for 6 People
15 minutes 20 minutes 6-8 servings Approximately $12.00

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved lengthwise
  • 1 large head broccoli, cut into bite-sized florets
  • 1 bunch thin asparagus, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 3-4 cloves garlic, sliced or minced
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • Optional: lemon juice, grated parmesan cheese, or balsamic glaze

Preparation Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Prepare the vegetables. Halve the Brussels sprouts, cut broccoli into small florets, and slice the asparagus into 2-inch pieces.
  3. Place all vegetables on a large rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and add garlic, thyme or rosemary, salt, and pepper. Toss well to evenly coat.
  4. Spread the vegetables into a single layer with the Brussels sprouts cut-side down.
  5. Roast for 20 minutes, stirring halfway through cooking, until vegetables are tender and lightly browned.
  6. Serve immediately. Optional toppings include fresh lemon juice, grated parmesan cheese, or balsamic glaze.
