Creamy Tuscan Chicken Thighs

Servings: 6

Estimated Cost for 6 People: $16-$18 total (~$2.75-$3.00 per serving)

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Season chicken thighs with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  2. Heat olive oil and butter in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Sear chicken 5-7 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked to 165°F.
  4. Remove chicken and set aside.
  5. Add garlic and sun-dried tomatoes to the skillet and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Reduce heat and stir in heavy cream.
  7. Add Parmesan cheese and stir until melted.
  8. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  9. Return chicken and juices to the skillet and simmer for 2 minutes.
  10. Serve warm.

One-Pan Lemon-Garlic Chicken Breast & Potatoes

Servings: 6

Estimated Cost for 6 People: $14-$16 total (~$2.35-$2.70 per serving)

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, diced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp butter

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Toss potatoes with half the marinade.
  4. Roast potatoes on a sheet pan for 25 minutes.
  5. Toss chicken breasts and green beans with the remaining marinade.
  6. Remove the sheet pan from the oven.
  7. Push potatoes to one side and add chicken and green beans.
  8. Dot vegetables with butter.
  9. Bake for 15-20 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
  10. Serve immediately.
