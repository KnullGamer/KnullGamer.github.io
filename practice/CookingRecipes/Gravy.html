Classic Savory Gravy

Recipe Information

Recipe Details
Prep Time Cook Time Servings Difficulty Estimated Cost for 6 Servings
5 minutes 20 minutes 3 servings Easy Approximately $4.50

Ingredients

  • 5 tablespoons butter
  • 5 tablespoons rice flour (or all-purpose flour)
  • 3 to 4.5 cups warm stock (chicken, beef, turkey, or vegetable)
  • 1½ teaspoons dried sage
  • Salt to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper to taste

Preparation Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a small non-stick saucepan over medium-low heat. Whisk in the flour and stir continuously for about 4 minutes until the mixture becomes smooth and golden brown.
  2. Add 1 cup of warm stock while whisking constantly until smooth. Continue adding the remaining stock one cup at a time, whisking continuously until the desired consistency is reached.
  3. Bring the gravy to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat and cook for 3 minutes while stirring constantly.
  4. Add the dried sage, salt, and pepper. Reduce heat to low and simmer for an additional 2 minutes. Serve hot.
