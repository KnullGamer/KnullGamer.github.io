Pan-Seared New York Strip with Caramelized Onions & Garlic Butter

Recipe Information

Servings: 6

Estimated Cost for 6 People: $45.00 ($7.50 per serving)

Ingredients

  • 6 New York strip steaks (8 oz each)
  • 2 large sweet or yellow onions, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1½ teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 4 sprigs fresh thyme

Preparation Directions

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil and 2 tablespoons butter in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add sliced onions and cook for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and caramelized. Remove and set aside.
  3. Season both sides of the steaks with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika.
  4. Increase heat to medium-high and add the remaining olive oil.
  5. Sear steaks for 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare, adjusting cooking time as desired.
  6. During the final minute of cooking, add the remaining butter, minced garlic, and thyme to the skillet.
  7. Spoon the melted garlic butter over the steaks continuously while they finish cooking.
  8. Transfer steaks to a plate and let rest for 5 minutes.
  9. Top each steak with caramelized onions and serve.
