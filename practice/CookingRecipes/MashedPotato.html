Classic Creamy Buttered Mashed Potatoes

A rich, fluffy, and classic mashed potato side dish.

Yield & Estimated Cost

Servings: 6 (approximately 1 cup each)

Total Estimated Cost: $3.78

Cost Per Serving: $0.63

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs Russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 1 cup whole milk, warmed
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt (for boiling water)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Preparation Directions

  1. Boil the potatoes: Place potatoes in a large pot and cover with cold water by at least 1 inch. Add kosher salt, bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 15–20 minutes until fork-tender.
  2. Drain and steam-dry: Drain thoroughly and return potatoes to the warm pot over low heat for 1–2 minutes to remove excess moisture.
  3. Mash: Remove from heat and mash potatoes until no large lumps remain.
  4. Incorporate dairy: Add butter and half the warm milk. Gradually add the remaining milk while stirring until creamy.
  5. Season and serve: Add salt and black pepper to taste. Whip vigorously for 15 seconds and serve immediately.
